# Needs you and Activity

Use **Needs you** (`/todo`, `G` then `T`) for decisions that are waiting on you, not for every change in the workspace. Choose **By kind** to review similar decisions together or **By work tree** to see decisions grouped by their parent work. Open an item to inspect its context and available actions. Add a reason when declining or returning something so the next person knows what to fix. If an item is waiting on another one, check the blocker before acting.

When enabled policy acts automatically, use **Handled for you** to see what it decided and why. Where offered, **Return to me**, **Return to Review**, or **Return to candidates** puts the item back on a human decision path; follow the reason prompt when shown.

Use **Activity** (`/inbox`, `G` then `I`) to catch up on what happened. Switch between **all** and **unread**, or use **Mark all read**. Marking a notification read is not a decision about the underlying work. If a notification needs action, follow its link or use **What needs you** to get back to the queue.
